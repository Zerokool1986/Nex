# NEX Master Context & Philosophical Foundation

## 1. What is NEX?
NEX is an integrated personal computing architecture designed to replace the centralized SaaS/Cloud paradigm with an autonomous, user-owned computing substrate.

## 2. Why Does NEX Exist?
In the status quo:
- Your files live on corporate servers (Google Drive, Dropbox, OneDrive).
- Your messages are brokered and mined by centralized platforms (WhatsApp, Slack, Discord).
- Your identity is leased to you by identity providers (Google Login, Apple ID, Phone numbers).
- Your personal location, photos, and private records are monetized or vulnerable to single-point-of-failure censorship and surveillance.

NEX restores **digital self-determination** by providing every human with an autonomous software appliance that runs locally, stores data redundantly across family/friend grids, communicates peer-to-peer, and shares compute deterministically.

## 3. The Sovereignty Axioms
- **Data Sovereignty:** My data is stored on my devices first, encrypted with keys only I control.
- **Identity Sovereignty:** I am my cryptographic key pair. No corporation can revoke my identity.
- **Relational Sovereignty:** My groups, chats, and shared spaces are private cryptographic enclaves.
- **Economic Sovereignty:** Resource sharing is accounted for bilaterally and cooperatively without corporate rent extraction.
