   - **Storage & Sync:** Monitor CAS storage usage, trigger anti-entropy synchronization, manage local quotas.
   - **Economics & Providers:** Manage attached payment rails (cards, bank, crypto), view subscriptions, track resource credits.
   - **Transports & Relays:** Monitor active network conduits (LAN, WebRTC, Reticulum radio), manage NAT relay peers.
   - **Social Recovery:** Manage M-of-N Shamir recovery shard distribution and trusted contacts.
